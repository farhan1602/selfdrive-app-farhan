How these numbers are calculated

Revenue is the invoice value of completed bookings (rental after surge and discounts, plus extra km, late fees, fuel and cleaning, minus any additional discount), counted on the return date. Fastag is a toll pass-through and is not counted as revenue. The security deposit is never revenue.

Cash follows the money: the advance, the balance and deposit collected at handover, and the final settlement at return (customer pays, or a refund goes out). Net cash in a period can differ from revenue because bookings are paid before and after the trip. Cash received includes security deposits, which are adjusted or refunded at return.

Utilisation is booked days (confirmed, picked up and completed bookings) divided by active vehicles times days in the period.

Limits: advances on cancelled bookings are not tracked (refunded or kept is not recorded). Bookings confirmed before this update have no recorded advance date, so their advance is dated on the day the booking was created.
